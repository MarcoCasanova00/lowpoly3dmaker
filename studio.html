◈ retro-qd3d studio

A modern remake of Apple's 3D Movie Maker v1.0 (1998) — pick a low-poly model → slap your own photos on it → pick a spin → export a looping embed for Neocities. Everything stays in your browser. Drag on the preview to rotate.

1 · Model Select (orig: click a model / drop a .3df — here: click / drop .obj)

2 · Textures (orig: drop PICT on a part — here: pick a slot, upload a photo; auto 16-bit + power-of-2)

3 · Animation (orig: 4 choices — same 4 here)

4 · Movie options → Export (orig: size/compression/bg → .mov)

5 · Shape (resize the model — width / height / thickness)

IRL workflow: photograph something you own (book, CD, box, framed print) in flat light → upload the photo(s) into the matching slots → export. The canvas embed repeats the 360° turn while LOOP is checked; uncheck it to play once (use ↻ Replay to re-watch). For the recorded .webm, looping comes from the loop attribute — use “Copy <video> tag” to get it right.
